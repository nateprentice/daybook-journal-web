# Personal journal pilot

These controls are available in Settings. They do not import personal information or turn on encryption automatically.

1. **Switch / create journal:** close other Daybook tabs first. The existing data stays in Original journal. Create an empty Personal or Test journal, then connect the intended Google account there. The active journal and account appear in the header. Separate journals have separate local entries, settings, history, backups and recording databases. They share this browser's optional vault passphrase. A journal is not a separate operating-system account.
2. **Preview Google import:** sign in, select calendars/task lists, date range and completed/undated options. Preview the copies and select entries before adding them. Suspected duplicates start unchecked. More results are shown in batches of 100; unseen results are not selected automatically. Import is limited to 3,000 preview results and 366 days. Original Google items are unchanged; imported copies can sync to Daybook's dedicated resources. Use Choose calendars for display without copying.
3. **Review possible duplicates:** matching title, kind and date/time are suggestions. Keep both or explicitly cross out an extra copy. Contents remain available; nothing is deleted automatically.
4. **Sync & change history:** review recent sync summaries and the last 50 entry changes, bounded to 350 KB. Undo creates a safety copy and refuses to overwrite a newer edit or orphan linked entries. Large entries require a backup restore instead. Emails, invitations, downloads and other outside effects cannot be undone here.
5. **Offline readiness:** prepare files while online and wait for Ready. Reopening uses a complete cached app copy; local edits queue until Google reconnects. Browser storage clearing/eviction, private mode and unsupported browsers can remove offline readiness. Google sign-in, fresh Google data and downloading a speech model still require network. Preparing the app does not download a transcription model.
6. **Backup reminders:** choose 7, 14 or 30 days, or defer until tomorrow. A prepared download is not proof the file was saved or restored. Keep a copy outside browser storage. Restore shows added, changed and removed entry counts before replacing this journal, and creates a safety copy first. Recording files need their separate downloads.
7. **Change vault passphrase:** available after unlocking the device vault. Enter the current and new passphrase yourself. All journals in this browser share it. New AES-256-GCM key wrapping uses PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA-256 with 600,000 iterations and a new random salt. The data key stays unchanged to preserve recording compatibility. Old exports still need the old passphrase; changing it does not revoke copies or keys already obtained by someone else. Google Calendar/Tasks and user-opened exports are outside the local vault.
8. **Collection templates:** Collections → Collection templates, or New collection in a collection picker. Reading list, Project plan, Meeting notes and Habit tracker create editable linked prompts. They do not invent tracker measurements or force undated ideas onto Today.

Validation: 287 Flutter tests passed, including journal isolation, stale-tab rejection, restore comparison, guarded undo, read-only Google previews, import idempotence, templates, backup reminders, passphrase rotation/failure recovery and small-screen layouts at enlarged text. Browser-bridge tests cover auth, audio/vault integration and a simulated offline cold restart with failed-update fallback. Physical iOS/iPadOS/native builds and loss of real network on every browser are not certified by these tests. This remains a prototype; begin with a small, backed-up personal pilot.
