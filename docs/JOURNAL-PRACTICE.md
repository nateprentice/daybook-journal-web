# Journal practice and a one-month trial

Today now offers Dashboard and Bullet Journal. The choice is remembered on this device. Dashboard keeps the collapsible Heads up and matrix; Bullet Journal shows one daily stream with a bullet key, previous/next day, completion/cross-out states, and links to current schedules. Capture in a selected historical day uses that day as the assigned date. New entries also retain their actual capture timestamp.

Rescheduling retains the previous assigned day. Original daily records remain reachable through Index and link to the current day or Future Log. Completed and crossed-out entries remain visible. Earlier capture timestamps cannot be reconstructed for legacy entries; the app does not invent them. Old entry dates are preserved from the first reschedule after this update. Word exports also include retained daily history. Entry content remains shared between views; migration markers are not frozen copies of earlier wording.

Calendar → Monthly Log → Monthly migration walks through unfinished prior/undated tasks, the selected month’s commitments, and an optional monthly intention. Choose Carry into this month to remove the old due day and carry it as a month-only plan; choose Schedule / organize for another date or collection; Cross out retains its optional reason. Leave unchanged is deliberate and does not move anything. Continue can leave items for a later review. No bulk migration or Google appointment creation happens automatically.

Morning intention and Evening reflection save optional notes, usable in existing collection, annotation and scheduling workflows. Opening a daily reflection again edits the same note. If its content changes remotely while the editor is open, the app asks you to reopen rather than overwrite the newer text. These are journal notes synced through private Drive app data, not Calendar appointments or Google Tasks. No additional Google permissions are needed for the practice features.

## Release checks

Automated coverage includes: original dates after local and incoming remote changes; JSON backup/restore; offline edits and simulated Google reconnect; same-ID updates; month-only carry decisions; timed tasks in daily review; reflection cancellation and editing without duplicates; Word history; 390px screens with 2× text; and the existing sync conflict, transport, recovery, storage and 1,000-entry tests.

The website is still a prototype. These tests do not prove physical iOS/Android operation, live multi-device Google recovery in every browser, closed-app background sync, or a cold website launch without internet. The new journal changes do not resolve those existing limitations. Keep a downloaded JSON backup; Word documents cannot restore the app.

## One-month trial

For a month, use the journal rather than expanding its feature set. Try a morning intention, capture short entries, and review in the evening when useful. Once a week, review unfinished work and export a backup. At month-end, run Monthly migration. Note confusing labels, repeated taps, missing information or interruptions to capture. Test restoring a backup on a separate browser profile before relying on it for important data. Broader release should wait for live Google/device checks and successful backup restoration.

No reminders or recurring tasks are created by this trial guide. The app remains usable without reflections, a matrix or Google sign-in.
